Solution Description

Building the Intelligence Layer for Workforce Transition

A shared data and decision model that transforms fragmented services into connected, measurable outcomes—starting where it matters most: the labour market feedback loop.

The Vision

One Data Layer Across the Entire Value Chain

Falck ELM Norway and Falck Karriere sit at every stage of the workforce transition journey—from preventing sick leave to reintegrating people into sustainable employment. Yet insights from one service never reach another.

We're building a shared information layer that allows AI-driven pattern recognition across services, letting advisors see what actually works, and giving Falck the ability to prove outcomes to NAV in ways no competitor can match.

Before we can share insights across services, we need a foundation. That means building the data pipeline where it's simplest to validate: at the point where users meet the labour market. Success here is binary and measurable—hired or not hired—and creates the feedback loop that makes everything upstream provable.

Strategic Focus

Why We Start at the Labour Market Endpoint

To know what works upstream, we first need to measure what happens at the destination. The job-oriented services are where 7-9,000 users meet the binary outcome NAV cares about most: sustainable employment.

01

Closest to NAV's Core Objective

Labour market participation is NAV's primary success metric. Starting here means we're building proof where it matters most for tenders and contracts.

02

Cleanest Data Requirements

Job-oriented services work with standard career data—skills, experience, goals—not sensitive health information. Lower GDPR barriers, faster AI training approval.

03

Validates Everything Upstream

Once we can prove what leads to employment, we can work backwards to validate which health interventions, clarifications, and follow-up actions actually contribute to outcomes.

Current State

AS-IS: Fragmented Journey, Lost Insights

Today, user activity dies on advisor desks, lives on personal computers, and reaches NAV as self-reported numbers. The actual labour market interaction is a black box.

Documents Die on Desks

CVs, applications, and assessments exist in scattered files across user computers and advisor folders. No structured capture, no reuse.

Self-Reported Activity Numbers

Users tell advisors how many applications they sent. No verification, no quality metrics, no direction analysis.

Labour Market Black Box

Once applications leave Falck's boundary, visibility ends. No tracking of employer responses, no pattern detection, no learning.

NAV Reports Without Proof

Advisors write narrative reports about user progress. No data-backed evidence of what interventions actually contributed to outcomes.

Future State

TO-BE: Seamless Data Flow, Captured Insights

User and market interactions flow through a unified system, giving advisors real-time visibility into job search activity, market response, and patterns that actually predict success.

Summary

Closing the Loop Changes Everything

For the first time, Falck will have verified data on what happens when users meet the labour market. Not self-reported application counts, but actual employer engagement—views, saves, contacts, conversions.

This creates the foundation for AI-driven insights that flow back to advisors in real-time, telling them which interventions, which skills, which approaches actually move people toward employment.

And because we start with the job-oriented services—where data sensitivity is lowest and the outcome is clearest—we can build the infrastructure that eventually validates every upstream service in Falck's ecosystem.

This positions Falck not just as a service provider, but as the only player in the Norwegian market who can actually prove what works.
