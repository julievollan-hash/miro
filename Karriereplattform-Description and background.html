Project Background

Closing the Feedback Loop

From insight to initiative: the context and rationale behind the Karriereplattform project.

From Insight to Action

Three Blind Spots

Our discovery work uncovered three structural weaknesses limiting the effectiveness of our employment services:

01

Information Gap

Over half of the information that determines participant success requires advisors to remember to ask and collect manually. We lack systematic access to the data that actually matters.

02

Time Loss

Advisors and participants spend hours on formatting, duplicate data entry, and manual reporting. A participant's birthdate may be entered up to 50 times across different documents and systems.

03

Missing Market Feedback

We send profiles into the market but have no systematic way to know if our work meets employer needs. We operate on assumptions—without documentation to validate them.

Core insight: We cannot document the effectiveness of our services because we have no closed feedback loop from the labor market back to our delivery.

The Missing Link

From Open Loop to Closed Loop

Todays NAV approved Felles-CV is a list of available candidates that goes out as a PDF newsletter to interested recruiters. The only response mechanism is email. We don't know who opens it, what they search for, or how to optimize profiles to to increase the response rate.

With a digital, searchable platform, we can finally capture what the market actually wants: traffic patterns, search queries, profile views, save rates, and conversion from interest to contact.

The Solution

One Platform, Three Perspectives

The Falck Job Seeker Platform integrates the participant journey, advisor workflow, and market feedback into a single system with one shared data core.

For Participants

Structured CV builder, gamified progress tracking, automatic reminders, and visible market interest in their profile.

For Advisors

Unified dashboard, automatic data collection (70% of currently manual tasks), semi-automated reports, and direct contact requests from employers.

For Market Intelligence

Searchable candidate portal with analytics: traffic, search patterns, profile views, save rates, and conversion tracking.

Strategic Position

AI-Ready, Not AI-Driven

The EU AI Act (effective August 2026) classifies employment services as high-risk. AI used for recruitment, candidate screening, or decisions affecting benefits eligibility requires extensive documentation, transparency, human oversight, and bias testing.

Phase Approach
Now Build systems right. Ensure data quality. Humans make all decisions.
Post-2026 Activate AI features when compliance framework is clear and data is validated.

We build the foundation before adding the turbocharger. This positions Falck as a responsible, forward-thinking partner—not a compliance risk.

What This Enables

From Assumptions to Evidence

For the first time, we can document the full chain:

Are our profiles reaching the market? — Track views, searches, and engagement

What competencies are actually in demand? — Capture search patterns and filters used

Where do we lose interest? — Analyze drop-off points in the funnel

What's our conversion rate? — Measure from profile view to meeting booked

The bottom line: We can finally demonstrate to NAV—and ourselves—whether our methods work, and adjust based on evidence rather than intuition.
