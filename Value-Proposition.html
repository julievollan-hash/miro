01
Situation

Supporting Norway's workforce through fragmented systems

Falck ELM Norway and Falck Karriere deliver labour- and health-market services on behalf of NAV and employers, helping people move from reduced work capacity or uncertainty about their future to clearer work goals, improved employability and, ideally, sustainable employment.

Today, NAV, Falck and employers all depend on fragmented, self-reported information and static documents—CVs, notes, reports—that do not give a continuous, verifiable picture of what participants actually do in the labour market during and after a program.

02
Complication

Scattered data weakens our ability to prove real impact

Because activity data, decisions and outcomes are scattered across systems, Falck struggles to document effect and cost-effectiveness in a way that is both credible for NAV and useful for day-to-day follow-up of participants.

This weakens Falck's ability to prove value in tenders, limits learning across services—health, clarification, follow-up, career—and makes it harder for advisors to see which interventions really move different users towards work.

03
Question

How do we connect fragmented processes into unified insight?

How can Falck ELM Norway and Falck Karriere connect the existing NAV processes, Falck assessments and participants' real job-search and work activities into one coherent, data-driven "before–during–after" model that:

• Strengthens documentation of outcomes for NAV

• Supports advisors in making better decisions in real time

• Positions Falck as the provider that actually owns the full value chain from prevention, via intervention, to reintegration into work

04
Answer

A shared information layer that turns activity into actionable intelligence

The project builds a shared information and decision layer that turns what Falck already does—NAV-aligned assessments, activity logging, career guidance and employer contact—into structured, reusable data that can be queried, visualised and reused across services and programs.

Master Journey Design

Design a master journey and data model around NAV's legal and logical phases, creating a unified framework for all participant interactions.

Service Mapping

Map Falck's services (before–during–after) onto the master journey, ensuring seamless integration across health, career, and follow-up services.

Reusable Tools

Implement tools that let advisors and participants capture activities once and reuse them many times—for follow-up, NAV reports, and outcome documentation.
